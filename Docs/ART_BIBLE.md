# Art bible

## Coastal crossing benchmark

Use the first supplied image for the angled view across a timber deck toward a grassy cliff landing. Its stacked ledges, darker water offshore and visible shallows establish depth. Use the second for timber joinery, rope wraps, lantern rhythm and warm wood against a bright coastal settlement. Create original silhouettes, motifs and pixel clusters from recipes. Reference images are review material only.

| Element | Construction direction | Review at gameplay zoom |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge | Vary plank length, grain clusters and end cuts within one wood ramp. Build beams, supports, rope sag and post bindings. | Deck direction and safe edges read in one glance. Posts cast contact shadows and feet meet the deck. |
| Cliffs and foliage | Build stepped ledges, broken vertical strata, cool crevices and grass overhangs. Group broad leaves and tufts around exposed rock. | Terraces remain distinguishable in shade. Foliage frames the landing and leaves the crossing clear. |
| Water | In URP Universal 3D use scene depth to vary transparent shallows, deeper teal and restrained shoreline foam. Check depth availability on the benchmark camera. | Submerged rock remains visible near shore. Foam follows geometry as the camera moves. |
| Character | Give the traveler an original silhouette, readable head and boots, a directional walk cycle and consistent equipment anchors. | At rest and in motion, feet retain ground contact. Foreground railings occlude the traveler consistently. |
| Light and camera | Angled orthographic follow camera, stable pixel scale, directional sunlight, cool ambient fill and contact shadows. | No distracting crawl, sprite blur or shadow flicker during a slow diagonal walk. |
| UI | Use original generated framed panels and icons; reserve bright accents for actionable states. | At target resolution text, silhouette and selection remain readable against sea and timber. |

Start with a proposed 32 texels per world unit and a small ramp per material. Review at 1280 × 720, then freeze the accepted density, palette, camera angle and zoom in versioned definitions. Use point sampling for pixel art and match world-space scale across sprites and surfaces. Mesh relief and authored pixel clusters carry the detail. A screen pixelation filter or permanent primitive scenery fails the benchmark.

Capture entrance, midspan and landing views plus a short movement pass. Keep the accepted captures with their recipe revision for subsequent reviews. Visual changes to accepted conventions require explicit review.
